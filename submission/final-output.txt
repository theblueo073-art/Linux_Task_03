1. grep

grep is used to search for specific words or patterns within text files. In this task, it is used to find lines containing ERROR and lines mentioning root or user01.

2. chown

chown is used to change the owner of a file or directory. In this task, it is used with sudo to change the owner of confidential.txt to root.

3. usermod

usermod is used to modify an existing Linux user's account settings. In this task, it is used to add forensic01 and guest1 to the sudo group.
 I learned that changing a file’s ownership can affect who is allowed to access or modify it. Even if a user created the file, they may lose access depending on the file’s ownership and permissions, which demonstrates the importance of proper ownership and permission management in Linux.
